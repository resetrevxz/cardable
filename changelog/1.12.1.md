# Cardable v1.12.1 — Smoother

Going to the inventory, opening a card and the first minute of the game no longer stutter.

- **The inventory opens and closes smoothly.** Each frame of the slide used to re-style the whole page and then measure it a second time. It now styles only what moves and measures once. On the test machine an open that dropped about a dozen frames drops none.
- **Opening a card is smooth.** Dimming the inventory behind the card re-styled the whole page on every frame of the lift. It no longer does.
- **The tutorial is light again.** Its pulsing highlight kept the page about half busy doing nothing; it is now a few per cent, which matters most on high-refresh displays.
- **Rare, Super Rare and Exotic cinematics start without a hitch.** Their sky, cloud and spiral textures were drawn in one go, some of them twice, at the moment the cinematic began (up to about 400 ms). They are now prepared in small steps while the pack tears open, once, and look exactly the same. Mythical and Ascendant compile their shaders together instead of one after another.

Saves are unchanged: no schema change. Nothing about how anything looks was changed on purpose, and Reduced motion, quality tiers and every setting behave as before.
