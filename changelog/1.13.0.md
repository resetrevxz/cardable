# Cardable v1.13.0 — Open doors

The Market is easier to read, easier to start with, and the Shop moved in.

- **The Shop is a Market tab.** The bag on the main menu opens the Market on its new Shop page: today's four cards and the featured pack, each bought through the same review as everything else in the Market, with what the card usually sells for between traders shown beside the Shop price. The classic showroom is one button away.
- **Your auctions stay on top.** While you bid on an auction or run one, a strip under the Market's tabs shows each of them: the card, whether you lead or were outbid, the price, and a ring that drains as time runs out.
- **What you win lands in your inventory, and says so.** A card you win, buy or swap for gets a card-shaped notification with a View button that opens it in your inventory. With the Market closed it flies from the notch into the inventory, which counts it in. A sale gets its own notice with the Credits it brought.
- **A short tour for new players.** The first time the Market opens, seven steps point at the real controls: Browse, Auctions, selling, the notch, Market time and the Shop. More → Take the tour plays it again.
- **Tips, icons and less text.** One short tip at a time on Browse and Auctions. Every page title, section and tab has an icon, the tabs' icons move when you arrive (the compass spins, the gavel strikes, the bag swings), and filler lines such as "Picked for you" are gone.
- **Listing a card is quicker.** Three ready prices as cards (Sell fast, Fair, Patient) with what you keep after the fee and the usual wait. Portfolio has a Sell button on every card.
- **Browse feels alive.** A refresh button, new listings that slide in with a New mark when you are at the top of the page, sellers and their avatars at the top of every tile, and reasons shown as small icon chips. Super Rare and better catch a holographic glint under the pointer.
- **Smarter traders with real names.** Traders look at their best leads first, bid less in crowded auctions, price just under the cheapest competing copy, favour auctions for cards many want, and send bargain hunters within the hour when something is listed well under its usual price (yours included). Every trader has a generated handle (GPUGoblin, FrameRateFran, neon_fox…).
- **Renamed.** Local is now **Standard** and Global Market is now **Players**.
- **Fixed: the notch flickered.** The pill re-animated and the open panel rebuilt itself on every clock tick; the Market's wallet and clock rebuilt themselves twice a second. They now change only the numbers that changed.
- **Fixed: the special-pack marker on the timer bar.** The next special pack and a special pack in your queue now sit inside the timer pill as small tags in that pack's colour, instead of hanging outside it.
- **The main menu.** The Market's storefront door swings open when you point at it or open it, and the Shop's bag swings.

Saves are unchanged: no schema change. Existing Standard worlds keep their traders, who take on their new handles once. Reduced motion, Hover animations off and the lowest qualities turn every new motion off.
