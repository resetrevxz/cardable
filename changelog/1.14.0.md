# Cardable v1.14.0 — One Market

Your collection and Credits now travel through one Market.

- **One wallet, one collection.** The game, Shop, computer traders and player trades use the same Credits and actual cards. There is no Standard/Players switch or separate online collection to import into.
- **Four clear tabs.** Browse, Shop, Your cards and Activity keep their names visible, with clearer text size and contrast. The Shop lives alongside trading; Market Settings groups related controls and keeps Advanced tools together.
- **Players join your feed.** Connect and link an account to include player listings alongside clearly identified computer traders. Offline play and computer traders remain available without an account.
- **Offline play stays yours.** After linking, a saved journal keeps actual card instances and Credit changes for ordered synchronization. Operation identities, receipts and an explicit active-device transfer guard against duplicate rewards and stale-device writes.
- **Simpler selling.** Bidding starts enabled and your preference is saved. Set an optional Buy now price that stays available after bids, or turn bidding off for a fixed price. Review the exact card, terms and fee before listing.

Migration keeps a durable backup and cancels stale queued listings with a readable notice in Account. Recovery codes offer an explicit restore of the last synchronized collection when the old device is unavailable, preserving the current save as a backup.

The app's save schema and identity are preserved. Legacy Market providers remain internal compatibility paths. Studio photos remain separate from JSON saves.
