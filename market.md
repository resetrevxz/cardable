# CARDABLE MARKET — MASTER PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
## Target: Cardable Market Beta 1.0.0

> **Status:** Owner-approved direction and working implementation brief, 12 October 2026. This is a planning specification for Claude, not a claim that the feature is implemented. Treat the eight stage prompts in `prompts/` as sequential execution briefs. Read the repository's **current** AGENTS.md, Designs.MD, ROADMAP, and relevant code before acting. The owner's newer Market instructions intentionally supersede legacy prohibitions on market, selling, accounts, networking, and audio only to the extent required by this scope; all unrelated preservation rules still apply.

## 0. Mission and quality bar

Turn Cardable, a premium GPU-card collector, into a collector-driven trading and market simulation game without diminishing its cards, pack reveals, inventory, offline availability, or performance. The market is a **core gameplay pillar**, not a generic e-commerce page. Design for **quality above quantity**: deeply implement, visually inspect, refine, test and finish each system before starting the next. A market that is dense with low-quality toggles fails the brief. Good details are more important than raw feature count, but the finished product should have deep mechanical scope.

The game must be playable wholly offline, including an engaging persistent simulated market. Online is a distinct, genuinely shared player-only exchange. Nothing about browsing offline mode requires Cloudflare, login or a network. No purchases involving real-world money, payments, cash-outs, crypto, financial products, or financial advice are in scope; Credits and Stocks are fictional game mechanics.

**Release target:** label the completed marketplace **Cardable Market Beta 1.0.0**. Keep beta market version separate from the host Cardable application's already-published version sequence (observed 1.5.6 on GitHub at planning time). Do not downgrade the host Electron updater or silently overwrite public app version to 1.0.0. A public beta release occurs only after stage 8 and validated integration. No interim public release from stages 1–7. Beta economy is **disposable**: clearly tell players that online balances, listings, migration claims and online inventory may reset before full launch; **never wipe their offline original saves/photos**.

## 2. Hard gameplay rules (owner decisions)

1. **Two environments**: `Simulation` (local, offline, bots only) and `Global Market` (online, real players only). Distinct balances, order histories, inventory claims, auctions and valuations. Never match an online purchase to a simulated local listing.
2. **No global bots for Beta**: no fake player listings, server NPC bidding, system wash trades, fabricated global transactions or synthetic online volume. The online exchange reflects actual player actions. An instant **Quick Sell to the game** is a transparent system redemption service, not a bot listing or player-to-player trade. Clearly label it and record it separately from market volume.
3. **Simulation freezes on app close**: bot orders, timers, auctions, generated news and price shifts advance only during active local gameplay, using active simulated time; on relaunch restore exactly, **no offline catch-up**. Online player auctions and orders run by server time and continue closing or settling while the game is closed.
4. **Volatility hybrid**: common, frequently traded cards tend toward stable values; scarce rarities/variants have more volatility and wider spreads. Avoid arbitrary wild price oscillations or easily exploitable deterministic arbitrage.
5. **Bots have persistent personalities**: collectors, bargain hunters, investors, quick flippers, completionists, risk-averse traders, hype followers and specialist collectors. Bots own budgets/inventories and pursue long-term goals; trades impact real simulated supply, demand and history. They are not LLM calls and do not need an online API.
6. **All existing eligible offline cards can participate in local simulation**. Provide **one-time beta legacy collection migration** into Global Market with safeguards, explicit player review, import receipt, stable deduplication and no damage to the original offline save. Because old offline saves can be edited, this is a **trust-based experimental beta import**, not cryptographically verified provenance. New online-eligible cards after migration must follow an explicit server-registered path; new purely offline cards remain offline unless a separately reviewed future conversion policy exists. Do not describe unverifiable imports as cheat-proof.
7. **Credits change**: remove +200 Credits per pack opening on newly processed pulls; no retroactive clawback from historical offline saves without opt-in migration. `Delete` becomes `Sell` where selling is intended, with instant Quick Sell and thoughtful **Bulk Sell**. Preserve separate discard semantics where an unsaved reveal truly must be rejected; show clear sale amount and exact-once receipt. Quick Sell pays far below a reasonable open-market expectation and is a **credit faucet**; market sales transfer Credits between players with a **2% fee** (rounding policy, proceeds preview and fee ledger). Consider economy sinks and inflation before setting rates. Do not double-count fees or payouts.
8. **Anonymous public identity**: generated **two-word pseudonyms** such as `Silent Falcon`, unique/stable per online account for beta; no real identities visible to other players. Backend still has private stable user IDs for ownership, accounts, reputation and security. Public profiles show completion history and reputation, not email, client IP or secrets. Design a regeneration/conflict solution that retains exactly two words.
9. **Listings are expressive**: users may upload **their own image**, including Director-mode exports, plus custom titles, optional descriptions, showcases and an artwork preview. Never let custom imagery obscure the real immutable card identity, variant, condition/metadata, serial and exact item(s) transferred. Allow comments and sale reputation. Plan image abuse safeguards even though beta skips sophisticated anticheat.
10. **Three core listing types**: fixed-price Buy Now, timed Auction, and card-for-card / card-plus-credit Swap. Support bundles, buy orders, counteroffers, seller-specified minimum relative value (%) and exact or flexible wanted-card rules. Include auto-bids, minimum increments, reserves, anti-sniping, optional buyout, bid escrow, settlement and history. **Simple mode** hides complexity; **Advanced mode** exposes detailed tuning including **custom auction duration**, which must **not** be accessible in Simple mode. Safe simple presets can still have standard timed auctions.
11. **Discover is not cheapest-first**: rank by purchase suitability relative to available balance, collections, watchlists, fair-value estimate, credibility of the valuation, demand/liquidity, popularity, freshness, opportunity and reputation. Prioritize **real player trades** ahead of local simulated suggestions in an optionally unified discovery presentation, keeping realm and settlement clearly separated. Explicit cheapest/most expensive sorts remain available but are not the personalized default. Add affordable recommendations as an algorithm, not simply a price <= balance filter.
12. **Stocks is intelligence, not stock ownership**: only graphs and analytics of collectible-card markets: Average Value, estimated fair value, target buying/selling ranges, best current bid, lowest ask, spread, trade volume, volatility, trending change %, liquidity, market depth, confidence, price histories, indices and potential opportunities. Do not implement tradable shares.
13. **Market values**: data-driven baseline per specific GPU; rarity, variant, frame/skin and provenance/serial characteristics can modify it. Simulation values incorporate bot sales; online values incorporate **only real completed player sales**, never local simulated trades, Quick Sell redemptions, bids or unexecuted listing prices. Early thin markets use labeled baseline/low-confidence estimates rather than fabricated volume. User wants price guidance beyond a raw lowest ask: target buy, target sell, urgency/time-to-sell and whether a listing is undervalued or overpriced.
14. **Settings**: market Simple/Advanced, preferred mode, notifications, feed preferences, chart type, default time window, trading confirmation level, display currency units, compact/comfortable density, privacy of profile comments where permitted, auto-bid notifications, low-data mode, reduced motion, default listing duration/preset, image upload quality, offline performance; integrate with existing settings architecture.

## 3. Experiences and information architecture

**Primary Market nav**: Discover, Auctions, Buy Now, Trades, Stocks, Portfolio, Activity (with global search, Wallet, My Listings, Watchlist, Notifications and Market Settings readily accessible). Distinguish the **realm/mode selector** prominently: Simulation and Global Market. Do not hide it in Settings. Market should feel like Cardable with a clean social browsing feed, not a spreadsheet skin everywhere.

### Discover
- For You, Following, Recently listed, Ending soon, Trending, Recently sold, Good value, Collection gaps, Around your budget, Rare finds, Popular variants and saved searches.
- Composable search facets: exact GPU model, generation, brand, rarity/multiple rarities, variant(s), skin, serial, serial range, original pack, card age, date, asking price, value%, value confidence, volume/liquidity, auction end, selling format, seller pseudonym and reputation, ownership status/wishlist, trade bundles, condition if it actually exists in source model. Do **not** invent collectible condition/edition data.
- Sorting: recommended, newest, ending soon, most bids, best value relative to confident estimate, highest/lowest price, most liquid, biggest discount, highest estimated profit, volume, popularity, rarity, serial, variant. Use clear sort labels, saved filter/search presets, recently used facets, filter chips, visible result counts, clear all, empty states and keyboard-friendly shortcuts. Preserve scroll/search state across opening detail/back navigation.
- Feed cards should show real collectible art, not only seller-uploaded covers. Cards get Average Value label and price-change indicators across detail and inventory; dynamic price data loads only when a realm is active and must not block offline pack opening.

### Listing/auction composer
- Start with choosing exact immutable owned card instance(s), support bundles and only compatible eligible ownership. **Never accidentally sell the entire finish stack** when one serial instance was selected.
- Listing type: Buy Now, Auction, Trade. Simple mode: suggested fair ask and time preset, estimated proceeds and obvious publish button. Advanced mode: reserve, minimum bid, increments, maximum auto-bid, duration custom field, allowed items, minimum offer % of Average Value, exact desired GPU/variant/rarity/serial, credit top-up, buyout, optional privacy/photo/copy and policy disclaimers.
- Support image upload from file picker and Director Gallery export, cropping, preview, aspect-ratio presets, alt description, replace/remove, upload progress, failure recovery and a neutral standard cover fallback. Include stock image rendering for other clients at low quality with lazy loading. Image is a **listing cover**, not evidence of ownership.
- Before publishing, display actual transfer item preview, pricing and 2% fee, Net Proceeds, source mode, listing expiration and reserved/locked card notice. Confirm/Undo where safe, show publish receipt, edit rules and re-list flow. Edits to price/duration on an auction with bids cannot undermine bid terms.

### Auctions and swaps
- Bidding: server checks valid owner, available funds, auction status and exact current price; applies minimum increment and proxy max, holds/release escrow correctly; closes per authoritative server time, extends late bids per policy, and settles exact-once. Concurrent bids, connection loss and time skew get deterministically resolved.
- Fixed purchase: compare-and-swap style atomic escrow/transfer across one authoritative transactional boundary or carefully designed saga; no double purchase, lost credit, duplicate card or charged failure.
- Buy orders: specific card families/variant conditions at limit price, quantity or exact card request, match by transparent priority rules (fair price/time), clear reserve, cancellation. Avoid creating commitments beyond wallet funds.
- Swaps: exact wanted cards or predicate rules (rarity/variant/minimum value %/serial), optional bid or credit difference, simultaneous locked ownership exchange, counteroffer, accept/decline/expire. No circular self-trading. Preserve serial and provenance.
- Anonymous profiles: two-word aliases, counts of completed successful sales, tenure in beta, rating distribution and moderated review comments. Avoid buyer doxxing and personal contact links.

### Stocks and portfolio
- Price chart timeframes 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d, all; line/candlestick where there are actual observations; volume histogram, bid/ask spread, market depth and reference source scope. Gainers/losers, rising demand, trending variants, cheapest relative to fair target, illiquid risk, and collection total based on **mark-to-estimate**, never guaranteed liquidation price.
- Metrics per card: `Average Value`, `Last Sale`, `Best Bid`, `Lowest Ask`, `24h Volume`, `7d Volume`, `24h/7d Change`, `Target Buy`, `Target Sell`, `Estimated Sale Window`, `Volatility`, `Liquidity`, `Confidence`. Clearly state `Not enough data`, `No sales yet`, stale cached values, and which realm is analyzed. Never show invented historical global trades.
- Portfolio: current valuation, credits, realized trading profit, unrealized value change when basis exists, fees paid, inventory exposure, owned listings, watchlist, saved comparison views, realized loss after fee, trade history and export. Charts and recommendation logic should be deterministic, inspectable and explainable.

### Quick Sell and Bulk Sell
- Rename Delete to **Sell** only in the contexts where a sale is real. During reveal, define irreversible decision and cancellation/keep flow carefully; don't erase a newly revealed card because the server is unreachable. Existing owned card sales select **one exact instance**.
- Bulk select by rarity, duplicates `keep N`, variant, favorites/locks exclusion, protected collections, value range, acquisition date, current filters, recommended safe selection; dry-run preview exact names/serials and per-card reward + total, no-sell exclusions, hold confirmation, partial failure representation and receipts/undo rules. A bulk sale must not cross realm boundaries.
- Quick Sell is a low, explicitly discounted bid from the *system*. In Online it is server-executed redemptions and not a fake player, not part of global marketplace sales volume. In Simulation it credits local ledger. **One way to bootstrap online wallet** is a capped one-time Quick Sell from eligible imported cards; avoid unbacked sign-up grants unless documented and limited. Tune actual Quick Sell percent/caps from balance tests; no magic cash from pack opening.

## 4. Economic design and algorithms

All values below are **proposed tunable models**, not finalized hardcoded pricing constants. Put prices, weights, thresholds, fees and RNG seeds in data and config, never UI.

- **Baseline**: `baseValue(cardId) × rarityFactor × variantFactor × cardSkinFactor × boundedSerialOrProvenanceFactor`. Most GPUs have distinct base values; never compute all same-tier cards at a single fixed price. Missing metadata must not invent collectible details.
- **Fair/average**: robust weighted recent *completed* transaction prices, segmented by economically interchangeable listings (same card/variant/skin and any actually meaningful rarity). Volume-weight recent sales, damp outliers, incorporate bid/ask only in clearly labeled indicative estimates if no liquid trades, and pull toward baseline under low samples. Separate realized last-sale, robust history metric and model fair estimate. Document formula, thresholds and confidence.
- **Volatility**: rarity-based target variance, bounded supply/demand shocks, mean-reversion strength and per-session trade impact. Rare cards may have wider spreads, big gains and losses; commons should be hard to manipulate. Do not use listing edit/cancel events as executed trades.
- **Opportunity score**: relative discount to fair price with confidence penalty, expected post-fee resale margin, liquidity, estimated time to fill and player's available budget; incorporate wishlists and collection gaps with user-controlled weights. No uniform cheapest-first ranking. Explain ranking in UI (`Fits your budget`, `Collection gap`, `Recent sales support value`, `Higher risk`). Never assume future profits.
- **Volume**: actual count and total Credits of *completed* player sales per period online, or completed simulation peer-to-peer bot/player sales offline. Exclude cancelled bids, self-trades, Quick Sell, fake backfills and uncompleted offers. Clearly distinguish units and credits volume.
- **Fees**: successful global market sales charge seller 2%, rounding to integer Credits according to a documented half-up/floor policy; previews match exact receipts. Apply fee policy consistently to auctions/buyouts/bundles/card-credit exchanges as applicable, and clearly show when a pure card swap has no credit-denominated fee. Wallet ledger must reconcile supply and sinks. Simulated 2% trades can mirror global for familiarity.
- **Economy testing**: run seeded simulations with thousands of orders, test newcomer ability to participate, wallet inflation and fee sink, card rarity stratification, scarcity, price collapses, manipulative corner cases, bots' budget depletion and transaction-per-second cost.

## 5. Offline simulator rules

- Simulation state must be **separately persisted** from primary card inventory and from online state, with versioned migrations, backup/export/restore or sidecar behavior consistent with existing save tools. Do not modify the raw primary Cardable save during simulator rollback. Prefer a local `market.sim` sidecar under established browser/Electron storage contracts; prove upgrades/import/export behave safely.
- Deterministic seeded PRNG and recorded simulation clock, active-session only. Creating bots/listings happens by event/rate budget, not per-frame/per-card loops. When closing, store simulator exact clock, wallets, inventories, active bids/orders, trades, valuation points and RNG state. Reopening shows same pending auction time, no phantom catch-up.
- Bot personality has horizon, preferences, bankroll, owned cards, target prices, risk appetite, purchase discipline, reaction times and inventory limits. Real market depth and liquidity simulated from persistent inventory. Bots may negotiate, miss auctions, bid, withdraw legal orders and seek varied cards, but cannot print credits or cards after generation beyond controlled simulated supply rules.
- Provide deterministic test fixture seeds, clear demo/reset **simulation-only** buttons and debug replay traces. Simulation is clearly labeled, optional market intro explains it does not interact with real people.

## 6. Online architecture (Cloudflare)

Recommended architecture, to be validated against account plan, build/runtime and current docs:

- Workers HTTP API for session/auth, listings/search, purchase, bids, swaps, valuations, history, notifications, media upload authorization and admin/beta maintenance.
- D1 for normalized accounts, alias map, approved imported card instances, wallets/immutable ledger, orders/listings, positions, sales, review comments, events, price aggregates, imports and idempotency keys. Index high-read market filters and pagination; do not rely on full scans.
- Durable Object(s) with SQLite-backed transactional storage for **specific contention domains** (e.g. auction coordinator and/or per-wallet transaction arbiter). Avoid unsafe cross-DO atomicity assumptions: select and document single authoritative write owner for each asset/credit transfer, or implement recoverable transactional state machine with escrow/invariant tests. Do not claim two separate databases are atomically committed by magic.
- R2 for sanitized, normalized, bounded public listing cover images; private/signed upload or direct upload with server verification, allowlists, size limits, EXIF stripping, thumbnail generation and lifecycle cleanup. The client must not control bucket object keys or gain arbitrary public write permissions. Creator uploads count toward quota.
- Queues/scheduled jobs only for expiry, asynchronous thumbnails, analytics and notifications; **no online bot market activity**. Expiry/bid settlement must remain correct even if queues are delayed or retried. Hibernating WebSockets optional for live auction ticks/notifications; polling fallback.
- **Auth**: accounts exist internally even while public identity is anonymous. Prefer low-friction secure login (email magic link or device code as feasible), without assuming Cloudflare Access for workforce is a turnkey public consumer-login solution. Desktop `file://` cannot embed Cloudflare Turnstile in place; if using Turnstile run it in an HTTPS authorization flow and validate token server-side. Protect sessions with secure token storage, expiration, revocation, rate-limits, CSRF/origin and input validation appropriate to auth scheme. Do not show user email in profiles.
- **CSP/renderer**: no general Internet dependency for existing gameplay. Opt-in network access restricted to allowlisted Workers and image endpoint; Electron navigation/preload changes narrow and auditable. No shell commands, system privileges, generic IPC, token exposure in source or compiled client. Avoid cross-origin breakage for `file://` and Electron.
- **Operational guardrails**: user explicitly authorizes Claude to operate the user's connected Cloudflare account and use normal billable resources needed to build beta. Use existing legitimate authorized login/session/Cloudflare CLI or MCP; never require copying account API tokens into chat or commits; use wrangler secrets and separate dev/staging/prod resources; inspect actual billing plan/quotas before deployments, make costs visible, set alerts/limits where available, do not silently enable uncontrolled paid plans, delete unrelated resources, register domains, or spend unbounded funds. Record actual created names, URLs, IDs **without secrets**, migrations and cleanup. If auth/cost confirmation is enforced by Cloudflare, stop the specific gated action and report it; continue unrelated work locally. No fake deployment success.
- **Performance**: cache public catalog and derived market data with freshness policy, cursor pagination, batched reads, backpressure/rate-limit, opportunistic WebSockets, downsampled charts and lazy image loads. Measure free-plan D1/Worker operations. Free plan limits can cause hard failures; gracefully show offline realm if online API unavailable.

## 7. Data contracts and consistency requirements

Plan explicit entities (names may be revised after code audit): `User`, `PublicAlias`, `Session`, `CardDefinition`, `CardInstance`, `RealmOwnership`, `Balance`, `WalletLedgerEntry`, `ImportClaim`, `Listing`, `ListingItem`, `Auction`, `Bid`, `Escrow`, `BuyOrder`, `SwapOffer`, `Sale`, `PriceObservation`, `MarketAggregate`, `Watchlist`, `Comment`, `Review`, `Notification`, `ListingMedia`, `IdempotencyRequest`, `AuditEvent`. Define unique IDs and immutable transfer history. Wallet balances are computed/validated against ledger and reserved amounts. No client authority over price paid, current bid, item ownership, fee, server time, balance or settlement state.

Atomicity invariants:
- A serial instance has exactly one owner in a given online realm, with at most one active listing escrow.
- A purchase settles once and moves exactly one item/set plus exact Credits, applying 2% exactly once.
- Buyer's reserved+available Credits never negative; bids/auto-bids that lose release holds.
- Two simultaneous purchases/auction closes/retries cannot duplicate cards, double-charge or double-credit.
- Same `idempotencyKey` and actor for a repeated request returns same result; conflicting payload fails explicitly.
- Local export/import cannot overwrite the server's online ledger, and beta wipe/reset never removes offline originals.
- Comments/media are sanitized; no raw arbitrary HTML, SVG script, XSS, giant uploads, spam flood or leaking private account IDs.
