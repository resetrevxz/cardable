# AGENTS.md — current Cardable working guide

Cardable's current app version is **1.14.0 — One Market** (publication status is recorded in `docs/MARKET-UNIFIED-1.14.0.md`); the series started at **1.0.0**, carrying forward the former 4.2.0 game without resetting saves. It is an offline GPU-card collector, delivered as a classic-script browser game and a Windows Electron app. The public repository is `resetrevxz/cardable`; its website is `https://resetrevxz.github.io/cardable/`. The card is the showstopper; the surrounding UI stays quiet.

## Runtime and gameplay boundaries

- Preserve offline `file://` play, classic script ordering and `window.Cardable`. No ES module conversion, runtime CDN or renderer network dependency. Bundle fonts, art and libraries locally.
- Installed Windows builds may use the approved native updater to check/download configured public releases and install on normal quit after a successful save flush. Development, browser and local previews stay offline. Follow [UPDATES](docs/UPDATES.md); never bundle credentials.
- **One Market (1.14.0) supersedes the historical realm and split-wallet contracts below.** The game, Shop, computer traders and player trades use one Credit balance and one collection. Account linking gates player listings in the combined feed; there is no visible Standard/Players realm switch and no separate online import flow. Before linking, offline play and computer traders continue. Linked offline mutations retain actual instances in a durable journal; ordered upload, idempotent writes, receipts and active-device transfer preserve exact-once ownership and balance changes. Do not replace actual rolled cards with catalog templates. `src/market/online/` owns renderer network access; network remains explicit and account-gated. Legacy `simulation`/`global` ids and provider APIs remain internal compatibility details, not permission to recreate separate economies. The four labelled tabs are Browse, Shop, Your cards and Activity; simple selling defaults to bidding, with a persisted preference and optional Buy now price. Advanced tools live in Market Settings. See [One Market](docs/MARKET-UNIFIED-1.14.0.md).
- **Historical Market evolution (1.8.0–1.13.0):** the following describes those releases only. Its separate realms, wallets, import and navigation rules are superseded by One Market above; unrelated invariants still apply. The owner's Cardable Market Beta brief (`market.md`, eight stages) supersedes the older "no market" rule on the `feature/market-beta` branch only, and only to the extent each stage needs. Stage 1 is interface and sample data: no network, no selling, no accounts, no changed Credits and no save writes. Stage 2 adds the offline Simulation: it may change the player's cards and Credits only through `C.marketSim` settlement (one save commit with a receipt, locked cards cannot be deleted), keeps its own world in a separate versioned localStorage record, and still has no network and no accounts. Simulated time runs only while the game is open, visible and not paused. Stage 3 made opening a pack pay no Credits: Credits come from `C.sales` (Quick Sell and Bulk Sell, one durable write with a receipt and a ledger entry) and Market trades; `C.valuation` answers what a card is worth. Stage 4 adds the online beta foundation: `src/market/online/` is the only code that may touch the network, and only after the player chooses Global Market and connects; with `apiBase` empty (the default until a verified deployment) the renderer CSP is `connect-src 'none'` and nothing is requested. The server is `cloudflare/` (beta resources named `cardable-market-beta*`, secrets only through Wrangler, never in the repo). Online Credits and cards are separate from the offline save, which an import reads and never changes. Stage 5 makes Global trading real: the server (`cloudflare/src/*`) is the only authority for ownership, balances, the fee, bid increments, deadlines and who won; the client sends intent and shows what came back, every write carries an `Idempotency-Key`, and Quick Sell is a disclosed system redemption that is never a trade, a price or a seller. No bots, fixtures or Simulation numbers ever appear in Global. Stage 6 adds analytics, the portfolio books and personalized discovery: every Global statistic comes only from completed authenticated player-to-player Credit sales of one card (`price_observations`), never from Quick Sell, quotes, offers, swaps, bundles or cancelled orders; cost basis is shown as "No cost basis" rather than zero; formulas, windows and caches are specified in `docs/market-beta/stage-6.md`, and benchmark data are labeled test fixtures that exist only in throw-away local databases. Stage 7 adds the social market: players upload pictures (JPEG or WebP, validated and stripped on the server, three sizes, private until attached to a listing, quotas per player) and get anonymous profiles, comments, reviews from completed trades only, follows and moderation tools; a picture or review never changes what is for sale, the operator's routes are server-side authorised and audited, and the Director album is only ever read. Stage 8 closes the beta as Market Beta 1.0.0 inside app 1.8.0: the acceptance record is `docs/market-beta/ACCEPTANCE.md`; the service has three modes (open, read-only, maintenance) set only by the operator or by the daily budget breaker, and read-only means a look changes nothing; a beta reset is dry-run only in code and is applied by hand after an export (`docs/market-beta/RUNBOOK.md`). Local Market 2.0 (app 1.9.0) reshapes the Simulation without changing its rules of conservation: its world gains news (brand, generation, rarity or card demand shifts the bots act on), fire sales and a market-maker persona; `src/market/sim/advisor.js` is the pure coach (suggestions, listing assessment, goals, net worth) that reads only the world and never changes it; the Simulation opens on Home; tuning lives in `src/data/market-sim.js` and `docs/market-beta/local-market-2.md` records the strategy experiment (`tools/sim-strategy.cjs`). Rarer rares (app 1.10.0) re-cut the pull odds, the Rare/Royal/Titan schedule, Market `rarityBase` and the top Shop prices together: change one and re-run `npm run odds:report` and `tools/check-value-relations.cjs` (the Shop must stay dearer than the Market for every card and finish); see `docs/RARITY-ODDS-1.10.0.md`. The same Cardable (app 1.11.0) restyles the Market into the main menu's own language: its header is the same in Local and Global (nothing moves or disappears), `src/ui/market/island.js` is the one pill that reports your trades and the clock, `src/ui/market/settle.js` plays the card's flight on `market:settled`, and rarity colour comes from each rarity's own opening colour (`ui.rarityRgb`); the rules are in Designs section 12 and `docs/MARKET-POLISH-1.11.0.md`. The notch (app 1.12.0) makes that pill a feed with settings: `src/market/notch.js` is pure (it reads a snapshot, never the world), `src/ui/market/island.js` draws it in the Market's header and, with the Market closed, on the main menu (it never creates a Local world and loads a saved one at start so the market moves while the game is open; `src/market/sim/game.js` holds the clock while a pack is opening, Studio or a tutorial is open, or setup or recovery is pending), every quick option opens the usual review, and what it covers, its order and how much it says are `marketNotch*` settings; see `docs/NOTCH-1.12.0.md`. Open doors (app 1.13.0) renames the realms Standard and Players (ids stay `simulation` and `global`), makes the Shop a Market tab (`src/ui/market/page-shop.js`, buying through `C.shopPurchase` behind the usual review; the menu bag opens it), adds the auction tracker and arrival notices (`tracker.js`), the first-visit tour (`tour.js`), icon motion, tips and fresh-listing motion (`flourish.js`), and trader strategies and generated handles (`C.data.marketSim.strategy` and `names`; handles come from a hash, never the world's random sequence). Market chrome that updates with the clock must rewrite only changed text, never rebuild (the notch flickered for that reason); see `docs/MARKET-REFRESH-1.13.0.md` and `tools/check-market-refresh-browser.cjs`. Cloud saves and audio stay disabled. Never read `marketValueUsd`; market values live in `src/data/market-config.js`. Market settings are `market*` keys in the shared schema; other Market preview records use their own storage key and never the save.
- Cards, rarities, generations, variants and packs belong in `src/data/` and their registries. UI consumes data rather than hard-coding game rules.
- Preserve stock/refill behavior, immutable serials, rolled variants and pack provenance. Reserve atomically before presentation; recovery and Keep/Delete must remain exact-once. A cinematic never rolls or pays again.
- Picker offers may have an empty `cards` array until confirmation. Do not mint unchosen offers or assume `pendingReveal.cards[0]` exists.
- Achievements are paused behind `C.config.flags.achievements = false`. The engine, data, UI and saved progress stay in the code and the save; the owner is reworking them by hand. Do not delete them or touch `state.achievements`.
- Do not bump the save schema for optional feature state. Normalize optional fields explicitly and preserve event-bus boundaries. Never silently wipe or replace saves.
- Preserve `com.cardable.game`, the primary `index.html` path, native user-data identity and separate Studio/photo IndexedDB blobs. JSON export is not a photo backup.

## Visual and performance rules

- Follow Designs and the actual CSS tokens. The owner now permits purposeful color accents in the default theme; monochrome is the optional saved theme, not a blanket chrome restriction. Keep readable shaded surfaces and obey the existing color/mono setting. Rarity, pack and artwork recipes remain listed in Designs. Inter and JetBrains Mono are the UI fonts; VT323 and Bodoni Moda have narrowly scoped artwork exceptions.
- Keep Very Low, Low, Medium and High, plus opt-in hardware-gated Very High, with High as the new-player default. The ten graphics controls are independent. Presets preserve FPS, reduced motion and other preferences.
- Use shared scheduling, focused full-card effects, bounded static inventory thumbnails and quality-aware canvas/resource budgets. Release hidden/offscreen work and temporary GL/material resources. Do not create a clock per card or an always-running idle loop.
- Respect reduced motion, keyboard access, focus restoration, touch controls and the existing Safe/Full cinematic gates. Saved preferences must not be changed by temporary battery/Safe Mode/adaptive overrides.
- Never write a custom property every frame on `<body>`, `<html>` or a container: it is inherited, so every element inside restyles each frame (the inventory, card detail and tutorial each did this and cost 5 to 20 ms a frame). Register it with `@property { inherits: false }` and write it on the elements that read it, or write the final style (transform, opacity) directly. Do not read layout (`offsetTop`, `clientWidth`) right after writing size in a frame loop. Build large procedural textures in sliced jobs ahead of the scene that needs them, not in its first frame.
- Do not promise universal frame rates or claim accessibility, photosensitivity or physical-device performance beyond what was actually measured.
