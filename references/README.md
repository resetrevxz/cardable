# references/

Put reference images here and describe each one below. Codex follows words better than pictures, so every image needs a "Copy" and an "Ignore" line.

Name files by purpose, for example `01-shine.png`, `02-card-frame.png`, `03-foil-macro.jpg`.

## Index

| File | Copy | Ignore |
|---|---|---|
| `01-shine.png` | The white light sweep across the card during the flip: angle, softness, speed, how it travels through the card. | Any colors (Cardable's shine is white), the artwork, text. |
| `02-card-frame.png` | The dark outline at the card edge, corner radius, edge treatment, proportions. | Card artwork, logos, text. |
| `03-…` | (add) | (add) |

## Tips

- Crop each image to the part that matters.
- Real photos of chrome/refractor foil cards at an angle teach the beam and iridescence better than screenshots of websites.
- Photos of GPUs (brushed metal, black shrouds, studio lighting) are good material references for card art and pack finishes.
- Use references for study and mood only. Never copy artwork, logos or branded designs from other games or companies; all card art and pack designs must be original.
