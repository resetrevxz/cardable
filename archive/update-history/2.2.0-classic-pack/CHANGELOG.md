# Cardable 2.2.0 — Classic Pack

- Classic Pack has a separate 5% chance on ordinary queue slots. The four branded packs retain their combined 5%; Rare still occupies every fourth opening. The first two tutorial openings stay Standard.
- All twelve ordinary tiers are eligible. Secret weight is ×4. Pre-2007 catalog hardware gets ×3 within its tier; modern hardware defaults to configurable ×1. Frame finishes are excluded; surface coatings remain eligible.
- Original aged-plastic case: Cardable sticker, drive bay, seven-segment display, controls, LEDs, vent grille and pixel badge. Ready, waiting, charge, dissolution and pull-tab opening share the existing scheduler and save transaction.
- Classic arrivals use an ordered-dither pixel tornado; lower quality and reduced motion shorten or replace travel with fades. Enter remains the opening fallback.
- Every Classic-pack card permanently receives a monitor bezel, tier LEDs and an amber terminal plate. Shelf, Grid, detail, reveal and collection copies retain it. Catalog-era tags and `era:classic` work for classic hardware from any pack.
- Schema 5 adds `cardSkinId`. Classic frames form separate stacks; ordinary card/finish stack keys remain unchanged. Offline VT323 is limited to terminal plates.

Royal and Titan requests were withdrawn and are excluded from this update.
