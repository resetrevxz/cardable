# Cardable 2.4.0 — Royal Pack

- Each scheduled Rare slot has a separate, identity-seeded 5% chance to become Royal. Ordinary slots and the first two tutorial openings retain their previous schedule. Royal can also be selected or forced through the existing dev registry.
- One card, Legendary through Secret only, with no extra tier weights: 50% Legendary, 37.5% Mythical, 10% Exotic, 2.25% Ascendant and 0.25% Secret under the current populated catalog and default luck. Finishes retain the existing gate.
- Original gold facet mosaic, detailed vector crown, red velvet/damask band, gold thread and beads, Cardable header and crown counter marker. Waiting is a translucent gold outline with pale-gold fluid; charge lights facets upward; dissolution floats gold triangles.
- `goldShine` replaces the incoming wrapper with one local diagonal sweep. `cutThenBox` retains the upper cut and guide, then raises a red lacquer holder with crown/trim and a Click hint after two seconds. Click or Enter opens the lid with a local shine and interior light.
- The committed tier's cinematic follows the box. If that tier has no cinematic, a three-second royal entrance raises the card with crown glint and gold shine. Recovery resumes the committed card directly, without repeating the box, pull or reward.
- Shared clocks, quality limits and reduced-motion alternatives; offline classic scripts. Schema 5 is retained. No new card frame, audio, market or Settings controls.
