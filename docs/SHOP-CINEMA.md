The featured pack sits in one continuous dark scene with an angled light beam, a thin floor rim and soft grounding. Large TITAN typography balances the real pack. Daily cards use their own silhouettes rather than repeated boxed display plates. Product copy keeps names, catalog facts, Credits, ownership and actual failure state. Slogans, exhibit captions, saved-quote labels and normal balance forecasts are removed; receipt identifiers are expandable.

Full motion carries a selected card or pack between browsing, inspection and acquisition through native view transitions. Buying still requires the same hold and successful durable write before acquisition. Reduced motion, Quiet and low animation quality skip the object transition. Unsupported browsers render directly. Closing, hidden documents and changed motion settings interrupt the transition; deferred callbacks cannot revive a closed view.

They use the existing saved settings schema. Sorting reorders the four existing DOM tiles, leaving their saved offers, quotes and purchase controls intact.

Repeated storage failure keeps the error visible. Failed holds now explicitly cancel their confirmation and show the exact failure message rather than leaving “Saving purchase…” visible when a disabled control loses focus.
