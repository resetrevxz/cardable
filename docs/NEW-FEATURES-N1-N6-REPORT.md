# Done

- **N1 — R flip:** the focused full card shares a 600 ms spring-eased turn controller across the revealed opening card, inventory detail and dev gallery. Repeats, modifier shortcuts, editable fields, dev controls, preferences and earlier opening phases are excluded. The detail button and gallery turn button use the same controller. A dim R keycap appears after reveal/detail, fades after four seconds, and returns on hover. Reduced motion crossfades. Hidden tabs pause turns, hints and back stamping; demotion to lite clears unfinished presentation.
- **N2 — common back:** every implemented tier uses the same near-black brushed body, neutral foil/beam/glare, keyline, edge lighting and pointer-responsive dot field. The engraved 28%-width c has an SVG inset shadow and thin highlight; a wide-tracked mono wordmark sits above it, with the serial below. The serial stamps on first display. The inner border is inset 12 px with corner notches. Dots occupy the existing art layer, preserving the ten-layer material stack. Unknown/unowned cards have no allocated serial to display.
- **N3 — inventory arrow:** a normal click toggles the sheet; a drag is distinguished by Euclidean pointer travel of at least 6 px. Only the generated drag click is suppressed, and a fresh press clears that suppression. Enter on the arrow toggles; I and ArrowUp open; Space does not activate it. The chevron rotates 180 degrees over 250 ms, while the existing physical sheet spring, menu scale/blur/dim and collection pulse remain.
- **N4 — carousel:** native scrolling/snap is replaced by a transform engine with one displayed position and target. Pixel/line/page wheel input, horizontal trackpad input, pointer drag/flick and keyboard navigation share it. Tracking uses critical damping, release snapping allows bounded overshoot of at most 6% of one pitch, and cards turn 34 degrees per step up to 50 degrees, recede in Z, scale to 0.82 and dim to 0.55. All shelf cards stay lite, including hover; detail alone becomes full. Normal virtualization is ±6. Reduced motion uses short linear slides without rotation or snap overshoot. The dev cadence sample now animates the shelf and uses unclamped elapsed frame time.
- **N5 — ordering:** All, By generation and By rarity reuse existing tile nodes with FLIP position offsets and interpolated dial poses. Old visible tiles stagger by 20 ms, with the animation capped at 900 ms; outside tiles jump to their new positions. Incoming views fade gently. Rapid reorder samples the current pose and position. The carousel recenters, the underline slides over 250 ms, and quiet group labels return afterward. Reduced motion uses a short fade.
- **N6 — version:** `config.version` is `1.0.0`. Dim mono `v1.0.0` appears beside Save & display on hover and in its settings header.
- Verification: **270 behavior groups** across the Stage 1–8, F1–F6 and new-feature suites; **six Stage 0 checks**; and **69 JavaScript syntax checks** pass. Existing tests were adapted to the explicitly requested all-lite shelf, transform scrolling and common Secret back. The random idle-logo check now waits for the configured maximum of two swaps plus its hold, removing a pre-existing timing flake without changing the logo implementation.
- Host CPU samples are recorded in `NEW-FEATURES-N1-N6-PROFILE.json`, including a moving 300-tile shelf and 300 tiles with one full Secret in detail. These instrumented Node measurements exclude browser layout, raster, blur and GPU compositing.
