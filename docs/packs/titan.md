# Titan: aluminium shroud

Reference: the Titan X and GTX 1080 Ti coolers: a faceted aluminium shroud, a window onto a black heatsink, chrome lettering and a cold LED edge. No brand logo.

## Front
- Polished crown plate with `TITAN` engraved in graphite, side chevrons, a chamfered window over a finned black heatsink, a bottom plate with hex screws.
- A cold LED line breathes on each side of the window.

## Back
- Black backplate with vent slits, screw grid and a spec label.

## Idle
The LED edge breathes and a bar of light crosses the fins every eight seconds. Both stop with the rest of the ambient motion.

## Hold
Heat rises through the fins as the hold fills and six ticks light on the crown plate.

## Opening (`shroudDial`)
Turn the machined knob clockwise through three clicks (or press Enter three times). Each click lights a latch. After the third, the shroud halves slide apart over a lit core and the reserved card follows. The arrival is `monolithDrop`.

No changes to probabilities, pack cadence, reservations, serials or rewards.
