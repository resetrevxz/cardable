# Classic: the beige tower

Reference: an FLP03-style industrial tower. Beige moulded plastic, two bay vents, a drive bezel, a control strip, a long intake grille, a maker's badge. The pack is a small box with real depth rather than a foil sleeve.

## Front
- Two louvred bay vents, a drive bezel with a slot and release lever (`cardable` is printed on the bezel).
- Control strip: red power rocker, key lock, RESET and TURBO buttons, a two-digit red seven-segment display and three indicator LEDs.
- Intake grille of 28 slats with two ribs; badge at the lower left; `SERIES 01` silk-screened beside it.
- A security seal across the lid with a tab and a pulsing hint, `PULL`.

## Back
- Fan guard, port row (DB-25, two DB-9, keyboard, power), five expansion brackets, serial label with barcode.

## Animations
- **Startup (`pixelAway`):** an ordinary Standard pack arrives, pixelates, and its blocks lift off and fly away, leaving the case.
- **Hold:** a brass key flies in from the lower right, seats in the lock and turns. The rocker glows, the display counts up, the HDD, turbo and power LEDs come alive and blink. The opened wrapper keeps the machine on.
- **Tear:** drag the seal tab up until it snaps; the case opens along its centre and the amber light shows the card.

## Card
Classic cards keep their permanent monitor-bezel skin, now with a fuller command panel: serial plate, DIP switch bank set from the serial, PWR/ACT/ERR indicators by tier, vent slots and a `C:\CARDABLE> SPEC` prompt in the terminal.

No changes to probabilities, pack cadence, reservations, serials or rewards.
