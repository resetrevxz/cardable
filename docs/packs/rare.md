# Rare: sapphire constellation

Lacquered sapphire with a drifting network of connected dots. Same layout as Standard; the dot field becomes the network.

## Front
- Body: deep sapphire gradient with a soft glow, chrome-blue crimps and a clearcoat that shows a sharp specular lobe and a softbox strip.
- Network: 26 nodes (the larger dots) drift on slow independent paths; any two within reach are joined by a line that fades with distance.
- Wordmark, `SERIES 01` and an `R+` ring, all in white.

## Back
- Sapphire body, centre fin seal, `RARE PACK`, `RARE OR BETTER`, `EVERY FOURTH PACK`, a barcode and batch code. The network keeps drifting on the visible face.

## Startup: the chameleon
The pack comes up as an ordinary black Standard pack, then changes colour like a chameleon: hexagonal scales flush teal and cyan from one patch of skin and settle sapphire, and the network and `R+` mark arrive last. It plays when Rare becomes the next pack, after a Keep, and once at launch when Rare is already next. Reduced motion and Very Low swap by a short cross-fade.

## Hold
As the liquid rises the grid assembles from the fluid line upward: nodes slide to the nearest crossing, lines give way to a clean white grid. Each printed mark (wordmark, series line, `R+`) breaks into grid-cell squares and re-forms, one after another, so everything has become the grid by the time the hold completes.

## Cut
Standard's cut, on the opened wrapper: the grid re-materialises once from the bottom as the cut begins, crossings brighten near the blade, and the dotted seam lights behind it.

## Quality
High and Medium animate the network at 30 Hz; Low and Very Low hold one pose. The grid and mosaic effects run only while holding.

No changes to probabilities, pack cadence, cutting strategy, reservations, serials or rewards.
