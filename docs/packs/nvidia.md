# NVIDIA: shroud and fan

Research: NVIDIA boxes are black with grey angular shroud lines and one green wrap-around band carrying the model name; the logo sits in a top corner.

## Front
- Black foil with two angular grey facets (the shroud lines) and a machined fan well in the middle.
- The eye mark sits on the fan hub; the wordmark sits in the top-right corner opposite `cardable`.
- Green wrap band (`NVIDIA PACK`) with a row of eight power LEDs above it.

## Back
- Quiet shroud lines, the same green band, fin seal, barcode and batch code.

## Animations
- **Startup (`rayTrace`):** a green ray sweeps down the pack, revealing it; below the ray the pack is still dark.
- **Hold:** the nine-blade fan spins up (motion ghosts past a third of the hold), glows green around the hub, and the LEDs light one by one. The band takes a bright sheen as the hold completes.
- **Cut:** a laser line follows the blade with white core and green glow; sparks fly from the tip and the seam dots ignite.
- Opened wrapper keeps the fan turning slowly with half the LEDs lit.

No changes to probabilities, pack cadence, cutting strategy, reservations, serials or rewards. Owner-authorized brand colour lives on the wrapper, queue marker and provenance only.
