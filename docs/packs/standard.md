# Standard: satin black

Satin-black foil with the Cardable dot grid parked on its right edge. One wordmark, one line of type, nothing else on the front.

## Front
- Body: near-black foil with pillow shading, fine grain and the shallow creases that fan from each crimp corner.
- Top crimp: vertical ridges, with a perforation row at the seam. This is the strip you cut.
- Wordmark `cardable` at the top left, tracked wide like the menu wordmark.
- Dot field: the same dot grid as the background, swelling and brightening toward the right edge, permanently. It stops short of the crimps.
- `SERIES 01` in mono at the bottom left.

## Back
- Same black body, the dot field mirrored to the left edge so the artwork reads as one wrapped surface.
- Centre fin seal running the full height.
- Small wordmark, `STANDARD PACK`, three lines of plain copy and a barcode with its batch code on the right half.

## Light
Reflections are cheap layered gradients positioned from each face's own normal: a wide soft environment, brushed micro-lines inside the sheen, a broad specular lobe and a Fresnel rim that strengthens as the face turns edge-on. The back has its own set. High adds a floor reflection that follows the turn.

## Opening
- Hold: the usual rising liquid; the dot field stays above it.
- Cut: the seam is a row of perforation dots. Dots swell and brighten behind the blade, a second faint row appears either side of the tip, and the row lifts away when the foil tears.
- Reduced motion: dots light without the lift.

No changes to probabilities, pack cadence, cutting strategy, reservations, serials or rewards.
