Cardable Global Market: Credit economy model (deterministic; card data and odds from the game)
Rules: Quick Sell pays 0.55 x baseline, rounded down, min 1. Fee 2% half-up, min 1, taken from the seller once. Credits are created only by Quick Sell.
Beta limits: 40 cards and 4000 Credits per account per UTC day; 250000 Credits per day for the whole realm.

4. Inflation: can Credits grow without bound?
   Not without more cards. Credits are created only by retiring a card at 0.55 x its value, a card can be redeemed once, and the beta creates no new cards online.
   So everything ever issued is bounded by the value of the collections players convert, times 0.55. A typical 300 card collection is worth about 9605 Credits to the system.
   Every Credit sale then destroys 2% (min 1). Total realm Credits can only fall after the last card is sold.
   The rate is bounded too: 4000/day per account and 250000/day for the realm.
   Steady state with each account issuing 600/day and each Credit changing hands 0.3 times a day: fees remove 4/day per account, so supply tracks issuance until the cards run out.

5. Abuse bound: a forged import
   An edited save can claim up to 3000 cards (beta cap). At the daily limit that is 4000 Credits/day per account; a farm of accounts is slowed by the per-address registration limit and bounded by the realm total of 250000/day.
   It is a beta risk, not a solved problem: the operator can revoke a disputed import, and the realm total is the circuit breaker.
