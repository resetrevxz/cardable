# Cinematics — current shared contracts

Presentation begins after the wrapper opening and reads the already reserved instance. It never pulls again, assigns another serial, consumes stock or pays another reward. Each unresolved card in a multi-card pack follows its own sequence. Descriptors live in `src/data/rarities.js`, `ascendant-cutscene.js` and `secret-cutscene.js`; timings below were inspected from those current data scripts. The byte-preserved [original RARITY-INTROS](../archive/4.2.0-cleanup/docs/RARITY-INTROS.md) retains the full incremental choreography and historical evidence; A/B interim endpoints are superseded by current C descriptors.

## Shared renderer and handoff

`src/fx/rarity-intro.js` owns the lazy bounded overlay, painter dispatch, clocks and retained backdrop. `cutscene-runtime.js` registers factories, descriptor sections, timeline mapping and exact-once beat events; `crystal-scene-engine.js`, `cutscene-screen-engine.js` and `cutscene-gl.js` share native shader/target utilities. Painters receive the existing opening scheduler; no private RAF, recurring timer, network asset or audio is added. Warmup occurs during committed dissolve/cutting, without changing the reservation.

At the descriptor handoff, mount the actual card back at its final position and enter the existing direct flip. The foreground fades through its first 160 ms (112 ms Fast). Ascendant holds metadata until its 1.5 s S8 materialization completes. All paths retain ordinary metadata/variant/Keep/Delete gates. `opening:introStart`, `opening:introSection`, `opening:introEnd`, `cutscene:beat` and quality events expose presentation, not generic completion/skip achievements. Last-frame dynamic beats flush before the renderer stops. Resets and save replacement release temporary nodes/GL resources.

The common controller exposes start/update/beginHandoff/updateRelease/stop/resize and backdrop restore/update/exit hooks. `backdrop:{enabled:true,exitMs:450}` mounts the field before foreground release; Keep/Delete fade it and clear it before the next card. Resize and live Mono/quality changes repaint without restarting the reserved story. Retained field updates are at most 10 Hz and sleep on blur, hidden/activity sleep or static policy. Hidden time is excluded when the shared clock resumes; Mythical/Exotic use analytic elapsed poses instead of the physics delta cap. The FPS cap, cinematic detail and canvas-resolution ceiling remain independent policy inputs.

Reload after commit exposes the saved front, finish and decisions directly, with the deterministic quiet field and no cinematic replay, 3D initialization, reroll or repeat reward. It does not persist ephemeral smoke/star time. Secret's live handoff preserves profile/field age/first inversion epoch; recovery instead uses the quiet seeded endpoint. Static shelf cards never borrow a live reveal session.

The shared skip hint appears after two seconds; the existing 500 ms black fade advances to the authored endpoint. Ascendant routes its climax through the single permitted envelope; Secret shows the quiet Found endpoint. Skip/reset cannot replay a single flash through backward seeking. A two-second hint does not unlock Keep before the normal readiness gates.

## Photosensitivity and quality

Strobing defaults to Safe. Full selection requires the existing three-second hold; each Full Secret playback has a pre-roll choice with an eight-second Safe fallback. `cutscene-safety.js` samples the final composed display at 64×36 in linear luminance, measures opposing large-area changes in wall-clock time, remeasures blended output and freezes prohibited reversals. Safe uses rate/calm gates; Full keeps at least 125 ms alternation intervals, 600 ms maximum bursts, 1,500 ms cooldown and an 8,000 ms total budget. OS artifacts use the narrower descriptor limits: up to four 350 ms E bursts within the global gate, at least 1,500 ms calm and no checker patches during bursts. The emergency brake and final-output limiter remain authoritative over Fast, scrub or authored beats.

Mono converts all backgrounds/highlights/materials. Reduced motion is independent of saved quality; lower tiers and Off keep understandable final card/Keep/Delete states. The dev luminance/flash meter is optional, samples at 10 Hz on the shared scheduler and is an implementation aid, not photosensitivity certification. No automatic profiling runs in normal play. Saved settings must not be permanently downgraded by adaptive cinematic quality, battery saver or one-session Safe Mode.
