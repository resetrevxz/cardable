Current builds are unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Download only from the official Cardable release linked here.

Saves, photos and backups

Use Cardable’s Data settings to export a JSON backup. Studio photos are separate from JSON saves. Download them from the Album too. Keep backups before replacing an installation. Reinstalling preserves the existing user-data identity.

Updates and older 4.x builds

The installed app checks the official release and downloads updates. A ready update installs on normal quit after saving. Full-folder builds update manually: close Cardable normally, then replace the app folder. Older 4.x builds need a one-time manual installer for the new 1.x series.

Graphics and performance

Very Low, Low, Medium and High are available in the game, with independent FPS and background-tab controls. Start at Medium and choose a lower tier if your device needs it.
